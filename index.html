The training OS for hybrid athletes.

One app for people who lift and run, row, ride, or flow — instead of one app for lifting, another for cardio, and Health for everything else.

Log everything, fast

A strength logger built for real training: supersets, drop sets, myo-reps, warm-up ramps, a plate calculator, and a live Apple Watch companion. Cardio gets real targets — rower /500m splits, interval pace bands, heart-rate zones. Yoga flows included.

Know when to push

A morning readiness score built from your overnight sleep, HRV, and resting heart rate, plus daily strain and a training-load comparison against your own baseline. ForgeFit shows a number only when it has the evidence to back it — until then it says so.

Your data stays yours

Local-first, no account required, no analytics, no backend. Optional iCloud sync for your training plan, an optional backup you control, and an opt-in community that never carries health or location data. Read the privacy policy.

ForgeFit is in active beta. If you're testing it — thank you; the support page explains the fastest ways to send feedback.
