Support

ForgeFit is currently in beta.

Testing the beta?

The fastest path is TestFlight itself: take a screenshot and share it straight to TestFlight, or open the ForgeFit entry in the TestFlight app and tap Send Beta Feedback. Screenshots with a sentence of context are the most useful reports — and numbers that look wrong are the most useful of all.

Found a bug, or have a question?

Open an issue on the ForgeFit issue tracker. Describe what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead; screenshots help.

Privacy questions

Start with the privacy policy — it is written to be read, not skimmed past. Anything it doesn't answer, ask on the issue tracker.

More contact options will appear here as ForgeFit approaches public release.
